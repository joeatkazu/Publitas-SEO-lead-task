> **Status: applied.** The restructure below was approved and is now in `3-proposal.md`,

Per instruction: sentences in `3-proposal.md` (and their twins in `DELIVERABLE.md`) that assert
the **structure** of the two-motion split are left unchanged. Their numbers are now wrong, but
fixing the numbers changes the argument, so the rewrite is yours to approve.

**Why the balance moved.** The old split was two-way: 68% vendor-own / 32% third-party, with
off-page owning the 32%. On the owner basis the 159 category-entry citations are:

| Owner | Cat-entry | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Competitor | 109 | 68.6% |
| UGC — YouTube, Reddit | 18 | 11.3% |
| Independent editorial | 17 | 10.7% |
| Adjacent vendor | 9 | 5.7% |
| Review platform | 5 | 3.1% |
| publitas.com | 1 | 0.6% |

Off-page shrinks **twice**. Only 31% sits outside competitor domains at all; and of that 31%, UGC
(11.3%) is participation and review platforms (3.1%) are review-velocity — neither is placement.
⚠️ **Correction (applied):** an earlier version of this file said the placeable editorial surface
was "26 citations across 11 domains." That paired two different populations. The 26 belongs to *all*
115 independent-editorial + adjacent-vendor domains; the 11 domains are the confirmed-absent subset,
and they carry **5** category-entry citations, not 26. Corrected sizing below.

So "two motions, run concurrently, because of the 68/32 split" is no longer the right sentence.
The honest structure is closer to **one main motion (on-page) plus three small distinct off-page
motions** — outreach, participation, review velocity — which is also what §3 of the research note
argued.

## The corrected tactic sizing (the version that shipped)

| Off-page tactic | Cat-entry citations, whole class | On pages Publitas is confirmed absent from |

columns are given so no reader has to infer which basis is in play.

(UGC 6 — YouTube 3, Reddit 3; independent-editorial 5 — kindlepreneur 3, prnewswire 2) against
publitas.com's **1**.

**The finding, stated plainly (corrected 2026-08-04):** the plan staffed the worked surface and
ignored the unworked one. Only 5 of independent editorial's 17 category-entry citations sit on
pages Publitas is absent from; the UGC surface's 18 are entirely unworked.

⚠️ The earlier "~3.5× larger" claim is **retired** — it compared editorial's absence-filtered 5
against UGC's class-total 18. On a single basis the two surfaces are near parity, 18 vs 17. Body
prose now runs on class totals throughout.
