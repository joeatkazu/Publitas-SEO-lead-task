================================================================================
YELP REVIEWS ANALYSIS SUMMARY
================================================================================

📈 CUSTOMER DESIRES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Interactive Page-Turning Reading Experience
   Reviewers consistently value the "magazine-like" experience that "mimics flipping through an actual magazine" — page turns instead of scrolling, giving publications a polished feel online rather than "just another PDF."

2. Reusable Templates for Consistent Branding
   The ability to build a template once and apply it across publications gives "a synced feel" and a "consistent look" for brochures, project packets, and magazines, cutting down setup time on every new project.

3. Table of Contents Navigation
   Both the automatic TOC generator and clickable page numbers are called out as standout features — reviewers can "click a page number ... to jump straight to that article," and the generator "has been awesome every single time we've used it."

4. AI Animation Enhancements
   Reviewers appreciate how AI animations "add a significant and valuable touch" to documents and "brings my books to life," making publications feel more engaging and interactive.

5. Hands-On, Responsive Customer Support
   Support is praised as "fast and outstanding," with one reviewer describing how a support rep "sent me a tutorial on how to move objects to the front/back using the layers panel" — going beyond standard help to teach practical workflows.

⚠️  PAIN POINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Confusing Tier and Page-Limit Transparency
   "The tiers weren't clear upfront about page limits," forcing one reviewer to "upgrade twice because we had 350+ pages" — they wanted all page-limit details laid out before paying.

2. Pricing/Upgrade Paywalls for Desired Features
   Multiple reviewers noted that "everything we'd like to try is always only available another tier," including custom URLs, fonts, and other customization options that small or self-funded organizations struggle to justify paying for.

3. Editing Workflow and Feature-Discovery Friction
   Post-publication editing is "not always intuitive" and requires "a bit of clicking around"; reviewers also mention limited keyboard shortcuts, and one asked for "guidance, training or encouragement" to understand the editing features.

4. AI Animations Can't Be Previewed Before Use
   The AI animation feature "sometimes totally transforms" characters into "other creatures," and there is no preview or written description before committing, making it risky for branded or illustrated content.

5. Large/Complex File Processing Is Slow
   When uploading "massive, high-resolution 3D design renderings or complex architectural drawing packages," the system "can sometimes feel a bit slow to process and optimize the files."
