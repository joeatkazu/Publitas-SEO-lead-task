YELP REVIEWS ANALYSIS SUMMARY
================================================================================

📈 CUSTOMER DESIRES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. **Ease of Use**
   Users consistently praise the platform's intuitive, user-friendly interface that requires minimal training or technical expertise. Reviews repeatedly highlight how simple it is to upload PDFs, navigate the back office, and publish catalogs quickly, with many noting they could launch without formal onboarding.

2. **Responsive Customer Support**
   Exceptional support is a recurring theme, with users describing the team as "stellar," "world-class," "accommodating," and "always helpful." Customers value that support staff proactively offer advice, walk through new features, provide additional training, and even reach out when they suspect issues with publications.

3. **Fast Publishing & Upload Speed**
   Users consistently value how quickly they can convert PDFs to online publications and get content live. Reviews highlight "one-click conversion," "catalogue uploaded in minutes," "fast uploads," and the ability to see results on the folder seconds after making changes, which accelerates their marketing workflows. **

⚠️  PAIN POINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. **Limited Analytics & Conversion Tracking**
   Multiple users express frustration with inadequate reporting capabilities, specifically struggling to track conversions from catalog views. Several mention needing external tools like GA4 or Looker for proper measurement, and one user notes that even after adding Publitas audiences to GA4, conversion data still doesn't display properly.

2. **Missing Bulk Operations & Batch Features**
   Users repeatedly request the ability to manage multiple catalogs or publications simultaneously—such as changing timings for several catalogs at once, scheduling in batches, or deleting multiple publications at a time. Currently, these tasks require repetitive single-item actions, which is time-consuming for users managing large volumes of content.

3. **Limited Customization & Editing Tools**
   Several users desire more control over design and content—including image editing capabilities (like adjusting brightness), alignment rules/coordinates for objects, more creative template options, and better tools for building dynamic templates. This suggests the platform's customization options feel constrained for users who want to differentiate their publications.
