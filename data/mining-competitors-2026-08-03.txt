================================================================================
YELP REVIEWS ANALYSIS SUMMARY
================================================================================

📈 CUSTOMER DESIRES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Simple and Intuitive Interface
   Users repeatedly praise platforms that are “easy to use,” “straightforward,” and quick to set up, saving time and reducing the learning curve.

2. Excellent Customer Support
   Fast, friendly, and responsive support is a recurring highlight, with many users describing support teams as helpful, attentive, and willing to go above and beyond.

3. Interactive, Polished Reader Experience
   Page-turning animations, table-of-contents navigation, embedded videos, and consistent branding are valued for making digital publications feel professional and engaging.

⚠️  PAIN POINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Pricing and Tier Transparency
   Several users find costs high, upgrades confusing, and desired features locked behind higher tiers, with page limits and customization options not always clear upfront.

2. Editing and Customization Limitations
   Complaints include non-intuitive post-publication editing, inability to preview AI animations, restricted URL customization, and limited options for personalizing content.

3. File Processing and Technical Performance
   Large file uploads can be slow, processing sometimes glitches, and issues with mobile responsiveness, file management, or multi-window use create frustration.
